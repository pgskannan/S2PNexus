# WF-001 – S2PNexus Workflow Engine

## Vision

S2PNexus Workflow Engine is an enterprise-grade, metadata-driven,
AI-enabled workflow and business process orchestration platform.

The engine is designed to support every S2PNexus capability including:

- Procurement
- Supplier Management
- Strategic Sourcing
- Contract Lifecycle Management
- Spend Analytics
- AI Orchestration
- Future business domains

---

## Goals

- Metadata Driven
- Multi-Tenant
