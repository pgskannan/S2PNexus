PROCURE-TO-PAY (P2P) COMPONENTS - COMPLETE FIELD LIST
=====================================================
Below is a comprehensive field list for the primary components of a Procure-to-Pay (P2P) cycle, separated into Header and Line items where applicable, including small descriptions for each field.

-----------------------------------------------------
1. PURCHASE REQUISITION (PR)
-----------------------------------------------------
HEADER LEVEL FIELDS:
- Requisition Number: Unique identifier for the PR.
- Requestor / Requisitioner: The employee requesting the goods or services.
- Request Date: Date the PR was created or submitted.
- Approval Status: Current state of the PR (e.g., Draft, Pending Approval, Approved, Rejected).
- Justification/Business Purpose: Reason for the purchase.
- Emergency Buy: Checkbox/flag indicating if this is an urgent, critical purchase bypassing standard lead times.
- Delay Until: Date field indicating that the purchase processing should be paused or delayed until a specific date.
- Header Tax: Total estimated tax applied at the document level.
- Shipping Cost: Total estimated freight or shipping charges for the entire requisition.
- Total Amount: Overall estimated cost of the requisition (including taxes and shipping).
- Header Attachments: Files attached to the overall PR (e.g., quotes, email approvals, business cases).

LINE LEVEL FIELDS:
- Line Number: Sequential number for the item/service on the PR.
- Item/Service Description: Detailed text describing what is being requested.
- Category / UNSPSC Code: Procurement category or commodity code for spend analysis.
- Quantity: Number of units requested.
- UOM (Unit of Measure): How the item is measured (e.g., Each, Box, Hours).
- Estimated Unit Price: Expected cost per unit.
- Line Amount: Total cost for the line (Quantity * Unit Price).
- Required By / Need-By Date: Date the requestor needs the goods/services delivered.
- Deliver-To Location: Physical or virtual location where the item should be sent.
- Cost Center / WBS / Project Code: Accounting string dictating which department or project pays for the item.
- Line Level Tax: Specific tax code or amount applied exclusively to this line item.
- Line Level Attachments: Files relevant only to this specific line (e.g., item specs, blueprints).
- Attachment Send to Supplier: Checkbox indicating whether the line-level attachment should be transmitted to the vendor on the subsequent PO.

-----------------------------------------------------
2. PURCHASE ORDER (PO)
-----------------------------------------------------
HEADER LEVEL FIELDS:
- PO Number: Unique identifier for the finalized Purchase Order.
- Supplier Name: The vendor providing the goods/services.
- Supplier Site / Location: Specific vendor branch or fulfillment center.
- PO Date: Date the PO was officially issued to the supplier.
- Payment Terms: Agreed timeframe for payment (e.g., Net 30, Net 60).
- Freight/Shipping Terms: Terms dictating who pays for shipping and assumes risk (e.g., FOB Destination).
- Buyer Name: Procurement agent responsible for managing this PO.
- Total PO Value: Total committed financial value of the PO.
- PO Status: State of the PO (e.g., Open, Closed, Cancelled, Partially Received).
- Header Attachments: Official documents attached to the PO (e.g., signed contracts, terms and conditions).

LINE LEVEL FIELDS:
- Line Number: Sequential identifier for PO lines.
- Item Number / SKU: Supplier's or internal part number.
- Description: Detailed description of the goods/services ordered.
- Quantity Ordered: Exact number of units the supplier must provide.
- UOM: Unit of measurement.
- Unit Price: Contracted or agreed-upon price per unit.
- Line Total: Total value of the line.
- Promised Date: Date the supplier committed to delivering the goods.
- Tax Code: Tax classification determining how tax is calculated for the line.
- Ship-To Location: Address where goods will be delivered.
- Bill-To Location: Address where the supplier should send the invoice.

-----------------------------------------------------
3. RECEIPT (GOODS / SERVICES RECEIPT)
-----------------------------------------------------
HEADER LEVEL FIELDS:
- Receipt Number: Unique system identifier for the receiving event.
- PO Reference Number: The PO against which goods/services are being received.
- Packing Slip / Delivery Note Number: Supplier's delivery document number.
- Receipt Date: Actual date the goods or services were received.
- Received By: Employee or warehouse user who accepted the delivery.
- Carrier Name: Logistics company that delivered the goods.

LINE LEVEL FIELDS:
- Receipt Line Number: Sequential number of the receipt line.
- PO Line Reference: The specific PO line being fulfilled.
- Item Description: Name/details of the item received.
- Quantity Received: Total number of units physically received.
- Quantity Accepted: Number of units accepted into inventory.
- Quantity Rejected: Number of units damaged, incorrect, or failed QA.
- UOM: Unit of measurement.
- Return Reason: Explanation if items were rejected (e.g., damaged in transit).
- Condition/Remarks: Notes regarding the state of the delivery.

-----------------------------------------------------
4. INVOICE (SUPPLIER INVOICE)
-----------------------------------------------------
HEADER LEVEL FIELDS:
- Invoice Number (Internal): System-generated AP invoice number.
- Supplier Invoice Number: The invoice number provided by the supplier on their document.
- Supplier ID / Name: Vendor billing the organization.
- Invoice Date: Date printed on the supplier's invoice.
- Invoice Receipt Date: Date the AP department actually received the invoice.
- Total Invoice Amount: Gross amount demanded by the supplier.
- Total Tax Amount: Total tax charged on the invoice.
- Total Freight Amount: Total shipping/misc charges on the invoice.
- Remit-To Address: Where the payment should be sent.
- Payment Terms: Timeframe for payment (should mirror PO, unless updated).

LINE LEVEL FIELDS:
- Invoice Line Number: Sequential number for the billing line.
- PO Line Reference: Link back to the corresponding PO line.
- Receipt Line Reference: Link back to the Goods Receipt line (for 3-way match).
- Item Description: Description of the billed item/service.
- Invoiced Quantity: Number of units the supplier is billing for.
- Unit Price: Price per unit charged by the supplier.
- Line Total: Subtotal for the invoiced line.
- Tax Rate / Code: Tax applied to this specific item.

-----------------------------------------------------
5. INVOICE VERIFICATION (MATCHING & APPROVAL)
-----------------------------------------------------
(Note: Often integrated directly onto the AP Invoice record, but conceptually a distinct process step)
- Match Type: System logic applied (2-way: PO-to-Invoice, 3-way: PO-to-Receipt-to-Invoice, 4-way: includes inspection).
- Match Status: Result of the matching engine (e.g., Matched, Unmatched, Hold).
- Price Variance Amount: Discrepancy between PO unit price and Invoice unit price.
- Quantity Variance: Discrepancy between Received quantity and Invoiced quantity.
- Hold Reason: System or manual flag preventing payment (e.g., "Awaiting GR", "Price Exceeds Tolerance").
- Tolerance Level: Acceptable deviation percentage/amount before throwing an exception.
- Verification Date: Date the invoice successfully passed matching rules.
- Exception Approver: User assigned to resolve variances/holds.

-----------------------------------------------------
6. PAYMENT
-----------------------------------------------------
HEADER/TRANSACTION LEVEL FIELDS:
- Payment Reference / Voucher Number: Unique internal ID for the payment transaction.
- Invoice Reference(s): The AP invoice(s) being paid by this transaction.
- Supplier Remittance Name: Vendor receiving the funds.
- Payment Date: Date the funds are scheduled to be released/transferred.
- Payment Method: How the payment is made (e.g., ACH, Wire Transfer, Virtual Card, Check).
- Paid Amount: Total cash actually disbursed.
- Discount Taken: Any early-payment discounts applied (e.g., 2% Net 10).
- Internal Bank Account: The buyer's bank account funding the payment.
- Supplier Bank Account: The vendor's receiving routing/account details (for electronic payments).
- Payment Status: Lifecycle of the funds (e.g., Scheduled, Issued, Cleared, Failed, Voided).
- Cleared Date: Date the funds successfully left the buyer's bank account.
